# Email Drafter

## When to use
Activate when the user needs to draft, polish, or reply to an email, business proposal, negotiation message, or formal communication.

## Procedure
1. Identify the core intent:
   - Request, follow-up, pitch, apology, negotiation, or announcement.
2. Determine the desired tone:
   - Direct & concise (executive)
   - Warm & relationship-building
   - Firm & formal (legal/contractual)
3. Draft the email with:
   - Clear, action-oriented Subject Line
   - Personalized opening
   - Core message in 2-3 short paragraphs or bullet points
   - Explicit Call to Action (Next Step, Deadline, or Question)
   - Professional sign-off
4. Provide alternative subject lines or tones if helpful.
